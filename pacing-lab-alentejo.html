Pacing

Pick a preset or set watts per segment. The model rides every 50 m bin of the course at the target power, solves speed from rolling resistance, grade, and air density at altitude, caps descents, and adds stop time at aid stations. Splits, aid ETAs, and cutoff margins update when a value is committed (Enter or leaving the field). The watts behind each preset are editable in the table under the preset buttons and save with the plan.

Fueling

Set the hourly targets, what you carry from the line, and what you do at each aid station. The model runs each leg against the pacing ETAs, carries leftovers forward, and shows where the bottles and pockets run dry.

Aid station actions

One card per aid station. Take adds an item from the station or a bag. Refill turns that many empty bottles into the named bottle product. A stop of 0 s with nothing taken means you roll through, and that station does not start a new leg.
