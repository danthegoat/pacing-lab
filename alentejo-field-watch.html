Race-day conditions

How to use it on the day

  • Your box. In 2025 the M40-44 box went at 09:05 with the women 40-44, one minute behind M35-39 and two behind M19-34; the boxes closed at 08:50. Expect the same shape.
  • The first 36 km decide the company. The 2025 riders at the line rode the whole day at an effective drag area of 0.10 to 0.18, which is a bunch, not a pair. Find the green-band bibs in the box and leave with them; the model says a small group is worth about seven minutes to you over the day, more than any pacing choice.
  • Checkpoints. ZA1 at km 36 and ZA2 at km 62 (the short-course split) are where the groups sort out. If the wheels around you at ZA2 are green-band bibs, you are riding the line; if they are the "just behind" bibs, the line is up the road.
  • Who not to chase. The front four ride under 4:00 and will be with the 35-39 box riders. Following them costs the day; the line group is the target.
  • Late entries. Re-pull the list in mid-October. Any of the twelve returners changes the band; a Roche or a Paegle at the front does not change your race, a Sancho or a Salvado in the line group does.

Caveats

  • Bibs come from the 22 Sep entry list; pending riders get a number when they pay. Verify against the start list at bib pick-up on the Saturday.
  • Modeled times come from a 2025 result where one exists, otherwise from a regression on public training rides (about 25 minutes one sigma per rider). Common Portuguese names may be matched to the wrong person.
  • The line itself is an order statistic over about 70 starters with a 10-to-90 band of twelve minutes; it moves with who shows up, not with the average rider.
