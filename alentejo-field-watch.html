How to use it on the day

    Caveats

    • Bibs come from the entry list; pending riders get a number when they pay. Verify against the start list at bib pick-up on the Saturday.
    • The group layer is a model of where riders' own pace puts them on the road, with a capture window for drafting. The window was tuned on the 2025 race, so the 2025 comparison is only a calibration check: its clocks landed within a minute there, its group sizes follow the window, and its names did not hold (no skill with 2025-style evidence, modest for riders with an own 2025 result). Use the cut group's clock and the bunch, and the names only to recognise wheels.
    • Modeled times come from a 2025 result where one exists, otherwise from a regression on public training rides (about 25 minutes one sigma per rider). Common Portuguese names may be matched to the wrong person.
    • The line itself is an order statistic over about 70 starters with a 10-to-90 band of twelve minutes; it moves with who shows up, not with the average rider.
